## Leadership Style

I build a high trust culture with open communication. We work hard but we make sure to celebrate wins and learn from our mistakes.<br><br>
You can often find my team unwinding with a round of Codenames or Jackbox games. We also love testing each other's knowledge of the team with trivia. 

## Team Impact

I love finding opportunities for each engineer to shine. I prefer promoting from within and encouraging my team to take true ownership.<br><br>
I step out of the way so people can grow. I am always ready to step back in when help is needed. I also encourage dedicated time for learning and development so my team can regularly showcase new skills.

## Technical Enablement

I view AI as a fantastic tool to help engineers act as true architects. It should enhance our skills rather than stagnate growth.
