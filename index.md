## 👋 Hi, I’m Mapi

I help teams build software that scales, behaves, and ideally doesn’t page anyone at 3 a.m. My leadership philosophy is pretty simple: treat people like people, create space for great ideas, and build a team environment where trust is the default, not an earned luxury.

Somewhere between campaign analytics and copy reviews, I fell in love with the little pockets of code I kept encountering. Now I’m an engineering manager who has navigated full stack work, cloud services, and even dipped into virtualization (unexpectedly fun). ## Leadership and Impact

As an engineering leader, I focus on balancing delivery speed, system reliability, and developer experience. I’ve led distributed teams shipping multi-platform SaaS products, improved CI/CD pipelines for faster, safer releases, and guided engineers through career growth and cross-functional collaboration.

### **Selected Highlights**

* **Platform Reliability:** Strengthened CI/CD pipelines and observability tooling, improving release stability and reducing regressions across multi-OS environments.  
* **Developer Productivity:** Unified fragmented backend services into a monorepo and built containerized preview environments, increasing developer velocity and test coverage.  
* **Team Growth:** Mentored engineers through 1:1s, feedback loops, and development plans, fostering accountability and technical leadership within distributed teams.
